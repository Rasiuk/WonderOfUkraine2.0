About us

Our team of experienced travel professionals is dedicated to providing personalized service to help you plan your dream vacation. We have extensive knowledge of Ukraine's culture, history, and geography, which allows us to create customized itineraries that showcase the best of our beautiful country.

Our tours

  • Lake Synevyr and Shypit waterfall

    • Departure from Lviv (07:00) / Stryi (08:00)
    • Pylypets (overview of Shypit waterfall and lunch)
    • Synevyr Pass (photographing and viewing of Carpathian panoramas)
    • "Synevyr" National Park (brown bear rehabilitation center and transition to the lake)
    • Stryi (21:00) / Lviv (return by 22:00)

    This very interesting excursion will introduce you to the most beautiful lake of the Carpathians, "Synevyr", and the impressive waterfall "Shypit", which is also located in the Ukrainian Carpathians. You will have the opportunity to try Soymivska and Kelechynska mineral springs, drinks and delicious dishes of Carpathian cuisine and, of course, admire the beautiful scenery of the unique Ukrainian Carpathians.

    Includes:

    • Transfer
    • Travel insurance
    • Professional guide
    • Travel insurance

    Price: 2000 UAH

  • Ancient History & Vibrant Present

    • Departure from Kyiv (08:00)
    • St. Sophia's Cathedral (iconic UNESCO World Heritage site)
    • Golden Gate (historical monument visit)
    • Bessarabsky Market (tasting local delicacies)
    • Kyiv Pechersk Lavra (exploration of the significant monastery)
    • Return to Kyiv by 20:00

    Walk through more than a thousand years of Kyiv's history in a single day. You will see the golden domes of St. Sophia's Cathedral, stand beneath the ancient Golden Gate, taste local delicacies at Bessarabsky Market and explore the caves and churches of the Pechersk Lavra, guided by a local expert.

    Includes:

    • Transfer
    • Travel insurance
    • Professional guide
    • Travel insurance

    Price: 3000 UAH

  • Black Sea Jewel

    • Departure from Odesa (08:00)
    • Primorsky Boulevard (city's iconic walk)
    • Potemkin Steps (historical site and great city views)
    • Odesa Opera and Ballet Theatre (exterior visit)
    • Lunch at a local seafood restaurant
    • Return to Odesa by 20:00

    Discover the pearl of the Black Sea: elegant boulevards, the famous Potemkin Steps and the grand Opera House. Enjoy the sea breeze, local seafood and the unique spirit of Odesa with an experienced guide.

    Includes:

    • Transfer
    • Travel insurance
    • Professional guide
    • Travel insurance

    Price: 3500 UAH
